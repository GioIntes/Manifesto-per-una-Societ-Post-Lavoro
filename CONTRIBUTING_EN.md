# Contribution Guidelines (English)

Thank you for your interest in contributing to the **From Automation to Emancipation: A Post-Work Society Manifesto**! This is an open project aiming to refine and evolve our post-work vision through a rigorous analytical framework.

---

### Core Guidelines
1. **Analytical & Systems-Oriented Approach:** Avoid purely ideological, demagogic, or emotional rhetoric. Arguments must be grounded in data, system efficiency, and provable logic.
2. **Systemic Consistency:** Proposed edits to existing sections must remain coherent with the technical-scientific premises set in Section I.
3. **Post-Work Focus:** The manifesto assumes automation is irreversible; proposals aiming to "tax robots to preserve human jobs" are considered out of scope.

---

### How to Participate
* **Issues:** Use them to report logical flaws, suggest new themes, or ask clarifying questions.
* **Pull Requests (PRs):**
  * For text fixes, formatting, or stylistic refactoring.
  * For improvements to mathematical syntax, data integration, or citations.
  * For refining translations in other languages.

For the Italian version of these guidelines, see [CONTRIBUTING_IT.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).
