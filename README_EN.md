### What is this project?
This manifesto presents an analytical, rational, and systems-oriented examination of the upcoming impact of Artificial Intelligence, Robotics, and full automation on society.

Unlike traditional political debates, this manifesto does not seek to slow down technological progress or protect obsolete jobs. Its goal is to redesign the socio-economic architecture (via a flourishing UBI, moving past private asset ownership, and fostering voluntary contribution) to transform the collapse of forced labor into the ultimate liberation of human time.

### Manifesto Outline
1. **Section I:** The Technological Reconfiguration (AI, Robotics, and Edge Computing)
2. **Section II:** The Crisis of the Work-Sustenance Model
3. **Section III:** The Architecture of UBI and Technological Dividend
4. **Section IV:** Cultural, Political Impact, and the Liberation of Time
5. **Section V:** Call to Action and Rational Conclusion
